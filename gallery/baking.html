I love food, but what excites me most is figuring out how to make something delicious myself.

My kitchen adventures began with cookies in elementary school and have since expanded to chocolate lava cakes, soufflés, mango mousse, cranberry cookies, borscht, steak, roast chicken, Thai tom yum soup, Mexican wraps, and, most recently, cinnamon apple pie.

I also happen to have a sensitive sense of taste and smell, which makes experimenting with flavors especially satisfying. I love inventing little desserts of my own and adjusting recipes to suit my family's preferences—a little less sweetness, a different texture, or just the right balance of flavors.

For me, cooking is a playful kind of creativity. It is part experiment, part sensory adventure, and part love language. There is something wonderfully satisfying about making something from scratch, then watching someone I love take that first bite.
