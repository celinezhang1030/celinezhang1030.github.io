I have always loved the magic of a good event: the anticipation before it begins, the little details that make it special, and the moment everyone forgets their usual routines and simply has fun.

Growing up, I was drawn to school musicals, lip-sync challenges, and all kinds of shared experiences. In high school, becoming Student Council President gave me the chance to move from being part of the excitement to creating it. From charity galas to Jumbo parties, I brought together aesthetics, creative concepts, and interactive elements to turn ideas into experiences my classmates could enjoy.

What excites me most is imagining an event from the audience’s perspective. What will catch someone’s eye? What will make them laugh? What will they remember afterward?

I love making things happen, but even more, I love creating moments that bring people together.
