I am the kind of person who might stop in the middle of a walk to pick up a particularly beautiful leaf, blow on a dandelion just to watch its seeds scatter, or take the long way home because something caught my curiosity.

My days are filled with little experiments and unexpected adventures: taking my dog to the park, trying a new sport, planting flowers, caring for my hedgehogs and turtles, or joining an elephant conservation camp. (I even got stepped on by an elephant once!)

I don't think every experience needs to lead somewhere or become an achievement. Sometimes, I just want to see what happens, learn something unexpected, or enjoy a moment for what it is.

There is so much to discover when you pay attention. I simply don't want to miss it.
