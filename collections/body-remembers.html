Music rarely stays in my ears for long before it finds its way into my feet. I began ballet at six, and over the years, I fell in love with the discipline and expressiveness of movement. Performing in Coppélia was a dream come true—especially the moment I finally stepped onstage wearing the tutu I had imagined for so long.

My back injury in ninth grade forced me to put dancing on hold. But my love for movement never disappeared. In high school, I tried K-pop choreography, discovering that learning a new style could be as challenging as it was entertaining. The movements were harder than they looked, and I was perfectly happy to be a beginner again.

Ballet taught me to appreciate precision; trying something new taught me not to take myself too seriously. For me, dance is not about getting every movement right. It is about finding joy in the process, expressing what words cannot, and letting the music move me in more ways than one.
