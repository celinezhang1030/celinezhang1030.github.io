The Stories Behind the Sport运动背后的故事

Interviews · 15 photos · 3 videos

✦

A lower-back injury from rowing first showed me how much an injury can change an athlete’s world. It sparked my curiosity about the stories behind the sport.

Since then, I have interviewed athletes from diverse backgrounds, including women and athletes with disabilities, exploring their journeys, challenges, and relationships with sport.

At the 2025 World Rowing Championships in Shanghai, I interviewed a Mexican rower with a high leg amputation. Her confidence challenged my assumptions and inspired me to look beyond competition. I began exploring the barriers athletes with disabilities face after retirement, from gaps in support to limited employment opportunities.

These conversations led me to create ParaPower, a social media platform sharing athletes’ stories and amplifying voices too often overlooked.
